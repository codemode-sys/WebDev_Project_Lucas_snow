Meet the founder

Hey, I’m Lucas.

Snowboarder. Winter sports enthusiast. Always chasing the next adventure.

A love of winter, shared.

I’m Lucas Snow, the person behind Snow Trails Travel Agency. I love snowboarding, winter sports and exploring snow-covered destinations.

Snow Trails is my way of sharing that excitement. The idea is simple: help people discover a winter adventure that matches their interests, whether they want time on the slopes or a chance to enjoy a snowy landscape.

For me, travel is also about the stories we bring back. This website brings destinations and those stories together in one easy-to-explore place.

The introduction is written for the fictional client in the assignment brief; additional wording is sample project copy.

The Snow Trails spirit

  • Adventure with a welcoming attitude.
  • Clear information without unnecessary clutter.
  • A love of snowboarding and winter landscapes.
  • Stories that celebrate the experience, not just the destination.
